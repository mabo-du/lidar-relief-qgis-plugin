## Why the full guide remains canonical

The complete guide ships inside the QGIS plugin and opens from its **Help**
buttons, so it is available offline and always matches the installed release.
This wiki landing page is intentionally small: it provides routes into that
guide without maintaining a second copy that could quietly become outdated.

All interface pictures in the guide are actual QGIS captures. Their capture
source and QGIS/plugin versions are recorded in the
[screenshot manifest](https://github.com/dig-tools/lidar-relief-qgis-plugin/blob/main/lidar_relief/docs/images/screenshot-manifest.json).

## Archaeological interpretation

LiDAR visualizations and automated detections reveal shapes and contrasts; they
do not establish a feature's origin, age, or significance. Compare independent
visualizations, historic mapping, field evidence, and appropriate specialist
advice before reaching a conclusion.
